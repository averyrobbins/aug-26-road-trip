Coordinates are rounded and approximate, not exact storefront positions. Points show the whole payment; a mixed basket’s primary type is not an item-by-item breakdown. No connecting lines or actual driven route are inferred.

Read the numbers with their limits.

Receipt totals are counted once. Category tax splits are approximations, not separately printed category-level taxes. Unknown dates and prices stay unknown. Available expenses are not proof that every trip cost is captured.

A public spending journal, not an anonymous dataset.

This page retains travel dates, merchants, cities and states, purchase amounts, fuel details, and approximate locations. Receipt images, item descriptions, exact addresses, purchase times, and payment identifiers are not included. The site is self-contained and has no analytics, tracking scripts, or external map requests. The hosting provider may still keep access logs.
